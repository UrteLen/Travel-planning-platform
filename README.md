# Travel-planning-platform
---
## Project Vision & Scope

This platform will be designed to help group of planners plan, budget and settle expenses for a shared trip. From choosing activities and creating a schedule to automatically figuring out who owes whom at the end. The main objective is to replace mix of chats, spreadsheets and separate expense splitting apps that groups currently use.
---

## 10 Core Features

1. **Inviting participants and managing permissions:** Joining the trip via a code/link, roles (organiser, participant, possibly ‘view-only’), permission restrictions (who can amend the budget, activities, etc.).
2. **Budget planning and forecasting:** Category budgets (transport, accommodation, food, activities), comparison of planned and actual expenditure, cost per person, alerts when limits are exceeded, calculation of the minimum budget for the entire trip.
3. **Sharing of joint expenses and optimisation of debt settlement:** Calculation of who has paid what and balancing accounts.
4. **Voting and prioritising activities:** A list of suggested activities, voting, and automatic suggestions on what to include in the plan based on votes, budget and free time.
5. **Activity schedule with conflict checking:** When adding an activity, the system checks the duration, working hours and travel time between locations, if any. If there are overlaps with other activities, the system alerts the user if it is physically impossible to complete them all.
6. **Recommendation system based on the selected destination:** Users will be able to select the city they are visiting, and the system will provide recommended places by category (food, accommodation, activities). Recommendations can be filtered by rating.
7. **Automatic daily itinerary optimisation:** An optimal order of visits is generated from the selected places, taking into account distances, opening hours, duration of visits, and the start and end of the day.
8. **Calendar and overall trip overview:** A visual overview of days and activities.
9. **Checklists and location review journal:** Preparation and packing lists; after a visit, comments, ratings and photos detailing what you liked or didn’t like about a specific location.
10. **Plan version history, automatic re-planning and summary:** Who changed the plan and when, comparison and restoration of versions without affecting other changes, when an activity is cancelled, the system suggests an alternative and a comprehensive trip summary (expenses, timetables, payments) with the option to export to PDF.
